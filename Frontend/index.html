INDUSTRY SPOTLIGHT

Understanding the
Petroleum Industry

Petroleum is one of the world's most important industrial resources. It provides energy, fuels transportation and supplies raw materials for thousands of everyday products.

🛢️

What does it produce?

Petroleum refineries produce petrol, diesel, aviation fuel, LPG, kerosene, lubricants, asphalt and petroleum coke. They also supply raw materials used to manufacture plastics, synthetic fibres, rubber, paints and chemicals.

⚙️

Raw materials and processes

The main raw material is crude oil, a naturally occurring mixture of hydrocarbons. Refineries may also use natural gas liquids and other hydrocarbon feedstocks. Major processes include distillation, cracking, reforming and treating.

🌫️

Air pollution

Refinery operations can release carbon dioxide, sulphur oxides, nitrogen oxides, particulate matter and volatile organic compounds. These emissions can contribute to climate change and poor air quality.

💧

Water pollution

Refining uses water for cooling and processing. Wastewater can contain oil, grease, ammonia, sulphides and other chemical pollutants. Improper treatment or discharge can harm aquatic life.

🏭

Other environmental impacts

Oil spills can contaminate soil and water. Refinery operations can also generate sludge, solid waste, noise and unpleasant odours. Extraction and transportation create additional environmental risks.

🌱

Path towards sustainability

Industries can reduce their environmental impact through renewable electricity, energy-efficient machinery, wastewater treatment, leak detection, emission-control systems and waste reduction.

Why PRAKRITI matters: Understanding industrial processes and their environmental impact is the first step towards identifying cleaner, more sustainable alternatives.
